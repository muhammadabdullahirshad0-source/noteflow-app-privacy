Welcome to NoteFlow ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines the handling of user data and permissions in the NoteFlow Android application (com.notesflow.muhammadabdullah).

1. 100% Offline & Zero Personal Data Collection

NoteFlow is built strictly with a privacy-first, local-first architecture:

  • All your notes, audio memos, drawings, checklists, and settings are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database (Room Database).
  • We do NOT operate cloud servers that collect, store, track, sell, or analyze your notes.
  • We do NOT require user account creation, phone numbers, or email sign-ins to use the app.

2. Permissions & Justification

NoteFlow only requests essential device permissions to provide features you explicitly interact with:

  • RECORD_AUDIO (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Used strictly when you tap the microphone button to record audio notes or voice memos. Audio files remain solely on your local device.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13+ only to deliver timely reminder alarms and scheduled task notifications set by you.
  • VIBRATE (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used to provide tactile haptic feedback during toolbar interactions and reminder alerts.

3. Data Security & Storage

  • All user-generated content resides within the application's private sandbox storage on Android. Other installed applications cannot access your private notes.
  • Backups: Exported JSON backup files are stored only in the directory chosen by the user and are never automatically uploaded to any third party.

4. Third-Party Trackers & Ads

  • NoteFlow does not bundle third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, or invasive analytics tools.
  • No device hardware identifiers (IMEI, MAC, Advertising ID) are tracked or shared.

5. Children’s Privacy

NoteFlow does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.
